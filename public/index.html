FAQ

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Which board is best for a beginner?+
Start with the H1 Glide — the EPP hull is forgiving on falls, the stance is wide, and the drainage-channel bottom keeps it steady while you build speed. If stability is your priority, the extra-wide 750mm G3 Cruise is the easiest board to learn on.
Do you ship worldwide?+
Yes. We ship from our EU warehouse (Poland), USA warehouse (Los Angeles), UK, Queensland (Australia) and Shenzhen (China). Most countries receive orders within 3–7 business days. WhatsApp us for country-specific shipping estimates.
What is the warranty policy?+
All WAVERIDER electric surfboards and e-foils carry a 1-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. Partner collection boats also carry a 1-year warranty. Contact WhatsApp support for claims.
How long does charging take?+
The G2 fast charges in 2 hours. The H1 takes 2–3 hours. The Y1 E-Foil takes 3 hours. All chargers included, supporting 110V and 220V.
Can I use an overseas warehouse to avoid import duties?+
We ship from 5 global warehouses: Poland (EU), Los Angeles (USA), Queensland (Australia), UK and Shenzhen (China). This often eliminates import complications. Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm your best route.
What is the difference between the F0 and F2?+
Both are carbon boards. The F0 Sport (65 KM/H, 16 KW) is the performance step-up for experienced riders. The F2 Apex (75 KM/H, 20 KW) is the full-carbon competition build with an integrated race battery.
Do I need experience to ride an E-Foil?+
No surfing experience required. The Y1 E-Foil has 24-gear speed control — start at walking pace and build gradually. Most riders achieve lift-off within 2–4 sessions.
What is the return policy?+
Returns accepted within 14 days of delivery for unused products in original packaging. Items showing signs of use cannot be returned. Defective units under warranty will be replaced or repaired.
