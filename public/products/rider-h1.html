Electric Surfboard

H1 GLIDE

The H1 Glide keeps powered surfing simple. Its buoyant EPP deck does much of the balancing while you learn the throttle, and at 22 KG the board is a manageable one-person carry from car to waterline. When the first battery runs down, the second drops straight in and the session keeps going — no charger queue on the beach.

Hull Design

HYDROVENT DRAINAGE

Water sitting on the hull is drag you carry everywhere. The HydroVent plate under the H1 Glide sheds it through stepped drainage channels, so the board unsticks from the surface sooner, planes cleanly, and turns in with less resistance. The plate integrates into the EPP structure itself, keeping the ride stable and predictable in flat water or chop.

Engineering

BUILT AROUND THE RIDER

  • Batteries drop into the bay and lock in a single motion — swaps take seconds and can be done on the water, without heading back to shore.
  • The quick-release fin comes off in about three seconds, letting you match the setup to the day's conditions without tools.
  • An LED display keeps remaining power in view mid-ride, and assist handles give you a solid hold for remounts and carries.
Construction

A HULL THAT TAKES HITS

EPP foam makes a forgiving core, but foam alone is not a finished hull. The H1 Glide wraps its core in an external fiber-reinforced skin: stiff enough to hold the board's shape under power, flexible enough to soak up knocks from docks, ramps, and hard landings. The surface is built to take an impact and keep its form, season after season.
