Deck Layout

THE EXTRA 30 CENTIMETRES GO A LONG WAY

Against the LUX 13, the 14 buys you a fixed rear bench with a real transom well behind it, flat double flooring underfoot and room for eight. It borrows its console and seating ideas from ZAR’s full-size Formenti range and shrinks them without the usual small-boat compromises.

Aluminium Hull

BIG-BOAT STANCE, TRAILER-BOAT WEIGHT

A 235 kg bare hull with 51 cm tubes gives the LUX 14 real presence in chop while staying inside easy towing weight. Four davit lifting points — two at the bow, two at the transom — make it a straightforward yacht companion.

Honda Power

UP TO 60 HORSES ON THE BACK

Supplied rigged with the Honda BF30 as standard, with BF40, BF50 and BF60 upgrades. Electric start and power tilt on every option, plus console steering, control cables, battery with isolator, fuel system and electric bilge pump — fitted before it reaches you.
